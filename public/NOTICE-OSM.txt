Retrieved 2026-10-06 via Overpass API. Adaptations: selected features,
local coordinate projection, distance compression to 1:4, simplified river
widths and symbolic landmark buildings. Plushuset and Åsbo use explicitly
approximate gameplay positions; the other six landmark positions carry
OpenStreetMap source IDs. This is not a surveyed map of Avesta.

Database rights are separate from rights in the game's code and artwork.

Version 0.8 adds oriented building rectangles derived from OSM footprints,
eight sourced landmark positions and five corrected district positions.
Facades, heights and the resistance courtyard are artistic/gameplay interpretations.
Road routing and safe access paths are adapted at runtime in shared/map.js.
