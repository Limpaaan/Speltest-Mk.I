Retrieved 2026-10-06 via Overpass API. Adaptations: selected features,
local coordinate projection, distance compression to 1:4, simplified river
widths and symbolic landmark buildings. Plushuset and Åsbo use explicitly
approximate gameplay positions; the other six landmark positions carry
OpenStreetMap source IDs. This is not a surveyed map of Avesta.
